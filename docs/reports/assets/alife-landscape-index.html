Method and limits

  • One CSV is the single source of truth; alife-comparison-stats.ts, alife-comparison-geometry.ts and alife-codeground-sensitivity.ts compute every statistic, and this document is regenerated from them by gen-alife-landscape-report.ts inside bun run gen:alife.
  • Cosmogonic's row is the code-grounded profile [4.0, 2.0, 3.4, 3.8, 4.5, 4.6, 4.4, 3.5, 4.0], re-audited against source and moved only by test-backed gates; the earlier self-score is kept solely as a labelled sensitivity baseline.
  • Peer rows are single-rater literature judgments with a stated confidence; they are not results of running each system. Anchors keep the scale consistent, but a second independent rater has not yet scored the matrix.
  • Survey statistics depend on which systems are included. Adding foundational models that score low on most axes (this pass added several) lowers the survey mean and raises Cosmogonic's z-scores without any change to Cosmogonic itself.
  • Not established by this index: sentience or consciousness, physical-quantum-hardware performance, or superiority over any specialist system on its own specialty.
