## 02  How to read the comparison

This is an evidence-audited landscape, not a league table. A cellular automaton, a neural-agent ecology, an evolutionary optimizer, a simulation library and a theory of consciousness answer different questions. Category is part of every record so an abstract theory is not penalized for lacking graphics and a toolkit is not credited with behaviors that a user has not implemented.


### Evidence labels

Primary-source reviewed: an original paper or abstract, creator documentation, original manual, or maintainer description supports the short factual summary. This does not certify every assertion in the original pasted basis text, full-text experimental quality, or present-day executability.

Partial-source reviewed: only limited primary text, bibliographic metadata, historical material or incomplete source access was available. The review note identifies the limit. Unsupported quantitative and superlative claims are not carried into the new description.

Unresolved: evidence is insufficient for a responsible counted entry. Such records remain visible in the workbook’s Dispositions sheet. A website being inaccessible is an access limitation, not proof a project is fictitious or defunct.


### Deduplication policy

One row represents one named research artifact, system, method or explicit family entry. UNIMAL is merged because it is the design space in the DERL study. Avida-ED, OpenELM, EvoSoro and Sensorimotor Lenia remain distinct tools or research extensions with related-family labels. Related families are navigation aids, not a count of independent inventions. Combined SDSR/Evoloop and Constructor/Sodarace rows each count once and name their differences.

Historical versions and publication descriptions are scoped to their sources. Original era values remain in Legacy scores, but are not republished as verified inception dates. A paper’s submission year, a software release year and a project’s origin year are different facts.


## 03  Why the old mathematics misleads

The repository’s breadth measure averages nine author-assigned ratings. Its current CQM vector is [4.0, 2.0, 3.4, 3.8, 4.5, 4.6, 4.4, 3.5, 4.0]. Their sum is 34.2 and 34.2 / 9 = 3.8. Peer maturity is reported separately, rather than included in this breadth average.

That arithmetic is reproducible. Its interpretation as scientific superiority is not justified: the ratings are subjective and approximately ordinal, the axes overlap, missing capabilities are mixed with out-of-scope capabilities, and the comparison population was chosen by the project. A decimal does not create measurement precision.

A z-score here describes distance from this hand-selected score table’s mean in its own standard-deviation units. It is not a p-value, a probability that CQM is better, or a confidence interval. PCA, distances and Pareto-front membership inherit the same scoring and selection assumptions.


### Sensitivity check on the original 129 rows

This is a diagnostic of rubric sensitivity using unchanged input ratings, before this audit’s exclusions and additions. It is not a replacement ranking. Removing consciousness-theory alone changes CQM from first to second; removing that axis and substrate-pluralism changes it to fourth.


### A better measurement model

Use separate, task-specific outcomes with definitions, units and uncertainty: inherited offspring fidelity; mutation and selection effects; lineage survival; novelty persistence; species and trophic diversity; control performance after ablation; wall time, memory and hardware. Record "not applicable" separately from "not documented" and "tested absent."

Rename substrate pluralism to coupled-model diversity when discussing classical software. Multiple differential equations, controllers and statevectors are multiple modeled formalisms on digital hardware, not evidence of multiple physical computational substrates. Treat consciousness-theory engagement as an architectural research topic, never a consciousness quantity.


## 04  Mechalogodrom: supported scope

The current working files describe a browser-hosted artificial-life and cognitive-mechanism testbed. Static inspection supports several concrete implementations, including inherited traits, bounded mutation, gene-linked fission and classical quantum-model calculations. Its most defensible distinction is the integration of diverse mechanisms with visible interaction and experimental instrumentation.


### Reproduction

Asexual trait inheritance, bounded point mutation and gene-linked fission probability in the live base population.

Boundary: Simulated organism copying and fecundity selection; not instruction-level self-replication, autonomous genome construction or demonstrated open-ended evolution.


### Open-endedness

Fission probability rises monotonically with inherited nW; the canonical script identifies the fixed optimum at nW=1.

Boundary: Bounded adaptation toward an authored objective. Sustained adaptive innovation and increasing organizational complexity remain unestablished.


### Ecology

Documented grazing, waste/fertilizer feedback, predation and sanctuary protection; refuge tests isolate the predation-immunity mechanism.

Boundary: Authored ecological feedback, not independently validated ecological fidelity or demonstrated long-term plant-genome coevolution. Test source inspected; outcomes not rerun.


### Morphology and visual scale

Reaction-diffusion patterns, state-driven morphing geometry and pooled rendering; documentation distinguishes 10k default scale from a 50k stress ceiling.

Boundary: Reactive morphing differs from evolved articulated body plans. Population count does not establish equivalent physics fidelity or smooth performance across systems.


### Cognition and learning

Bounded neural policies, prediction-error control, chemotaxis, numerical drive resolution and metacognition modules have focused intervention tests.

Boundary: These mechanisms do not define a calibrated intelligence score. Retained V4 and Phase-B failures restrict broader learning, adaptation and general-intelligence claims.


## Mechalogodrom: evidence boundaries


### Model integration

Classical statevector calculations, numerical wave dynamics, neural controllers and ecological rules supply interacting control signals.

Boundary: Multiple numerical model families share classical hardware. They establish neither physical substrate diversity nor QPU operation, quantum speedup or quantum consciousness.


### Instrumentation and replay

Seeded RNG diagnostics, ablation hooks, test artifacts and input replay provide observability and reproducibility tools.

Boundary: Tests and statistical randomness checks do not certify scientific validity, cryptographic randomness or physical entropy. Live entropy requires recorded inputs for exact replay.


### Consciousness research

Kernel outputs explicitly carry indicatorOnly; canonical documents record a project assessment of eight Butlin indicators met and six partial.

Boundary: An architectural interpretation, not external certification or evidence of subjective experience. Reported null-control and coupling limitations remain material.


### Scientific maturity

The repository publishes source, focused tests, retained negative results and a nine-axis comparison based on documentation judgments.

Boundary: Independent replication and matched competitive benchmarks are not established. The 3.8 mean and rank depend on a subjective rubric and selected comparison set.


### Xylos note

A separate recurrent numeric organism senses chemistry, adapts coupling and acts autonomously, with visible neural state and bounded local input recording.

Boundary: Apex indicator results do not transfer to Xylos. Replay reconstructs its input-driven brain trajectory, not the shared world; consciousness and open-ended evolution remain unestablished.


### Current negative results matter

The repository retains failed V4 and Phase-B controls and narrows positive Titan results to bounded game-policy causality. These results must remain beside positive mechanism descriptions; counting modules or successful tests cannot justify a broad intelligence, adaptation or consciousness claim. Experimental outcomes are repository reports and were not rerun here.


### Xylos belongs in the updated profile

Current Xylos documentation describes recurrent numerical sensing and action, plastic coupling, visible neural state, seeded operation and optional browser-entropy input. Its replay reconstructs local brain-input trajectories, not an entire world rollback. This is a meaningful subsystem update within CQM, rather than an extra external competitor or proof of sentience.

## 06  What would strengthen the claims

The following is a proposed validation program, not a claim that these experiments were run during this document audit. It turns the original score axes into reviewable research questions.

Reproduction and selection. Compare trait-linked fission against trait-blind fission and mutation-disabled controls, keeping resources and event streams matched. Track parentage, offspring traits, population size and selection response across independent seeds.

Open-endedness. Predefine novelty measures and adaptive challenges. Track innovation discovery and persistence over increasing run lengths and compute budgets, alongside stagnation baselines. Do not claim infinity from a finite run.

Ecological causality. Ablate refuge immunity, resource renewal and waste recycling separately. Measure persistence, diversity, interaction networks and energy accounting; report extinction cases as well as successful runs.

Cognition and learning. Separate inherited controller structure, online parameter updates and human-authored heuristics. Use held-out perturbations, matched controls, action ablation and transfer tasks; preserve currently failed controls.

Physics and numerical models. Check conservation and discretization behavior against analytic or trusted numerical references. Report time step, grid resolution, precision and model-coupling assumptions.

Reproducibility and scale. Pin code, configuration and hardware; save random seeds plus external entropy inputs. Measure throughput and memory under equivalent tasks. Publish raw measurements, analysis code and failed cases.

Independent evidence. Provide a minimal reproducible benchmark bundle and invite independent replication. A large internal test suite establishes software checks, not scientific construct validity or external acceptance.

Consciousness indicators should be reported with their theory-specific assumptions and uncertainty. The underlying research framework discusses computational indicator properties; it does not make an internal checklist a proof of experience. Butlin et al., 2023.
